## 1. Prerequisites (The Essential 3 Elements)

1. **A Primary Google Account**:
   - Both new and existing accounts work. An account configured with a US region profile directly activates the **Google AI Pro** tier (non-US accounts typically show as Google Plus, with identical core AI perks).
2. **A Payment Card with > $1 Balance**:
   - Any international credit/debit card (VISA / Mastercard) or compliant Web3 card (e.g., Bybit card);
   - Must have at least $1 balance to pass the temporary $1 pre-authorization hold (which is reversed immediately).
3. **Academic Student Credentials**:
   - Official enrollment verification document (e.g., CHSI academic verification report for students in Greater China, or official university enrollment PDF/portal screenshot).

## 2. Navigating the SheerID Academic Verification

SheerID handles global academic verification on behalf of Google. The key is correctly identifying your university in the institutional database.

### Step 1: Access the Student Promotional Portal
Navigate to the official student promo portal at [https://gemini.google/students/](https://gemini.google/students/) and click **[Get Student Offer]** ➔ **[Browse All Student Offers]**.

### Step 2: Country & University Search Strategy
If searching directly under your specific regional country name yields no results, select **"Taiwan"** in the Country/Region dropdown. The backend database under this regional category contains virtually all major accredited universities across Greater China and East Asia. Type your university's full native or official English name to select it from the predictive dropdown.

### Step 3: Fill Out Personal Details
- **First Name & Last Name**: Use the Latin/Pinyin spelling matching your student document;
- **Date of Birth**: Ensure this matches your official identification and student records;
- **Email Address**: Your Google account email;
- Click **[Verify My Student Status]**.

### Step 4: Upload Official Proof Document
Upload your official enrollment verification document in PDF or high-resolution image format. Ensure the document shows your full name, university name, academic year, and active enrollment status.

### Step 5: Verification Confirmation
SheerID's automated validation typically processes compliant academic certificates within **10 to 30 minutes**. You will receive an official confirmation email: `You've been verified for Google AI Pro Student!`

## 4. Critical Step: Cancel Auto-Renewal Immediately

The most common concern with 12-month free trials is forgetting to cancel before the full $19.99/month charge kicks in a year later.

**Key Rule: Canceling auto-renewal immediately will NOT terminate your 1-year trial!** Google retains your full benefits through the scheduled expiration date, and your account simply reverts to the standard free tier without any charges.

### Cancellation Steps:
1. Click your Google profile avatar ➔ **[Manage your Google Account]**;
2. Navigate to **[Payments & subscriptions]** on the left menu;
3. Select **[Manage subscriptions]** ➔ **[Google One]**;
4. Click **[Cancel subscription]** and confirm;
5. You will receive a confirmation stating that your benefits remain fully active through your scheduled expiration date.

## 5. Security Warning: The Patched "$8.99 Upgrade Bug"
