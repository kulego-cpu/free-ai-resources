# Why Google Gemini Is the Best Free AI API in the World

Among all tech giants offering free LLM quotas, Google's **Google AI Studio** stands out as the most generous platform for individual developers, students, and tech enthusiasts:

- 🎁 **1,500 Requests Refreshed Daily**: Resets automatically every midnight, fully sufficient for personal translation, coding assistance, and web tool usage.
- 🧠 **1 Million Context Window (1M Tokens)**: Feed an entire 500-page book or comprehensive technical documentation in a single prompt.
- 💳 **No Credit Card Required**: Zero international credit card barriers. Any regular Google account works out of the box.
- ⚡ **Gemini 2.5 / 1.5 Flash Flagship Models**: Ultra-fast first-token response, with latency under 600ms.

---

## 1. Prerequisites

1. A standard Google Account (Gmail is sufficient);
2. Direct network access to Google services.

---

## 2. 3 Steps to Claim Your Personal API Key

### Step 1: Open Google AI Studio Console
Open your browser and navigate to the official API key portal:
👉 [https://aistudio.google.com/app/apikey](https://aistudio.google.com/app/apikey)

If you are not logged in, enter your Google credentials. On your first visit, review and accept the terms of service.

### Step 2: Create Your API Key
Once inside the console:
1. Click the blue **"Create API key"** button in the top left;
2. Select **"Create API key in new project"** from the prompt;
3. The system will instantly generate a unique key starting with `AIza`.

### Step 3: Copy and Secure Your Key
Click the **"Copy"** icon next to the key string.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Security Notice:**
> Your API Key acts as your personal identity on Google Cloud. Keep it safe in your local notes and never publish it to public GitHub repositories or social media.

After copying your key, return to the **FreeAI Dog Homepage**:
1. In the provider bar above the toolbox, select **🔵 Google Gemini**;
2. Paste your `AIza...` key into the input field and click **"Save Config"**;
